About
E-Notes

E-Notes is a handwriting notebook made for studying with PDFs. Every PDF page gets its own notes margin; write with the S Pen, a stylus or a mouse, highlight, erase precisely, select with the lasso and use the free AI helper on your pages. Your notebooks sync live between Windows, Linux, Android and the web, and keep working offline.
