E-Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

E-Notes is a handwriting notebook for PDFs, created by Md. Towfiq Bin Hasan and TH team. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address (and, if you use “Sign in with Google”, the name and email your Google account shares). It is used only to sign you in and keep your notebooks separate from other people's.
  • Your notes: notebooks, handwriting, text boxes and the PDF files you add. They are stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in our database so they sync between your devices.

How it is protected

Data is stored with Supabase and transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each account can only read and change its own notebooks and PDFs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not show ads or track you across other websites.
  • Your notes are only sent to an AI service if you yourself use the optional “Ask Claude” feature, and only the sheet you ask about.

Deleting your data

You can delete notebooks inside the app at any time. To delete your whole account and all stored notes, open an issue on GitHub or contact the developer, and it will be removed.
